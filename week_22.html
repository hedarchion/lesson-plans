Listening for Specific Details: Hobbies & Free Time
    
      <span>1 Devotion</span> | 
      <span>Monday 2026-06-29</span> |

    Standards & Learning Outcome
    <p><strong>Content Standard (CS):</strong> 1.1 Understand meaning in a variety of familiar contexts</p>
    <p><strong>Learning Standard (LS):</strong> 1.1.2 Understand with little or no support specific information and details in simple longer texts on a range of familiar topics</p>
    <p><strong>Learning Outcome (LO):</strong> By the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to listen for specific details about hobbies and free time activities and complete a listening task with at least 4 out of 7 correct answers.</p>
    <p><strong>Theme:</strong> Hobbies & Free Time</p>
    <p><strong>Main Skill:</strong> Listening</p>
    <p><strong>CEFR Profile:</strong> Mostly A2 Revise, some A1. Simple vocabulary, sentence frames, and guided practice throughout.</p>
  

  
  
    Set Induction <span>5 min</span>
    <p><strong>Activity: Guess the Hobby!</strong></p>
    <p>Teacher plays <strong>3 short audio clips</strong> (5 seconds each) without revealing what they are. Students listen and guess the hobby.</p>
    
      Clip 1: Sound of a guitar string being plucked (hobby: <em>playing guitar / music</em>)
      Clip 2: Sound of a basketball bouncing on court (hobby: <em>playing basketball / sports</em>)
      Clip 3: Sound of pages turning and a pencil writing (hobby: <em>reading / drawing / writing</em>)
    
    <p>Teacher asks: <em>"What do you hear? What hobby is this?"</em></p>
    
      <strong>Scaffold for A1:</strong> Teacher prompts: "Is it music? Is it sports? Is it reading?" Students point or say yes/no.
    
    <p>Teacher elicits <strong>"hobby"</strong> and <strong>"free time"</strong> vocabulary and writes on board. Ties into today's lesson: <em>"Today we will listen for specific details about hobbies."</em></p>
  

  
  
    Lesson Development

    
    Explanation 1: Hobby Vocabulary <span>5 min</span>
    <p>Teacher introduces/revises key hobby vocabulary using picture flashcards (digital or printed):</p>
    
      <strong>playing football</strong> | <strong>reading books</strong> | <strong>watching TV</strong> | <strong>listening to music</strong>
      <strong>drawing / painting</strong> | <strong>playing video games</strong> | <strong>cycling</strong> | <strong>fishing</strong>
    
    <p>Teacher says each phrase aloud. Students repeat chorally, then individually. Teacher points to <span>Dharshayani</span>, <span>Ryyan</span>, <span>Wafy Hau</span>, and <span>Harith Aqasha</span> to say one hobby each.</p>
    
      <strong>Sentence frame on board:</strong> "In my free time, I like to _______."
    

    
      Activity 1: Listen and Point <span>5 min</span>
      <p>Teacher places picture cards of 6 hobbies on the board/table. Teacher says a sentence describing one hobby. Students point to the correct card.</p>
      <p><strong>Teacher says:</strong></p>
      
        "You use a ball. You kick it with friends." -> <em>playing football</em>
        "You sit quietly. You look at words and pictures." -> <em>reading books</em>
        "You sit in front of a screen. You use a controller." -> <em>playing video games</em>
      
      <p>Teacher nominates <span>Nur Amni Awatif</span> to point to one, <span>Nur Dayana Qhairena</span> to point to another, and <span>Nur Faqihah Humaira</span> to point to a third.</p>
      <p>Whole-class gesture game: Teacher says a hobby, students mime it (e.g., strumming a guitar for "playing music", dribbling for "playing basketball").</p>
    

    
    Explanation 2: Listening for Specific Details <span>5 min</span>
    <p>Teacher explains: <em>"When we listen, we can listen for <strong>specific details</strong> -- small pieces of information like <strong>who</strong>, <strong>what</strong>, <strong>when</strong>, <strong>where</strong>, and <strong>how often</strong>."</em></p>
    <p>On board, teacher draws a simple table:</p>
    
      
        Who?
        What hobby?
        When?
        How often?
      
      
        Afiq Ziyad
        plays football
        Saturday
        every week
      
    
    <p>Teacher models: <em>"I listen and I hear 'Afiq Ziyad plays football every Saturday.' I write: Who? <span>Afiq Ziyad</span>. What? plays football. When? Saturday. How often? every week."</em></p>
    
      <strong>Key phrases on board:</strong> every day, every week, on Saturday, on Sunday, in the morning, in the evening, twice a week, once a week.
    

    
      Activity 2: Listen and Complete the Table <span>8 min</span>
      <p><strong>Part A (Guided -- whole class):</strong> Teacher plays a short audio (or reads aloud) about two students:</p>
      <p>
        "Hi, I'm <span>Izzudin</span>. I like reading books. I read every evening after school. On Saturday, I play football with my friend <span>Nazrin</span>. We play in the field near my house."
      </p>
      <p>Teacher guides students to identify:</p>
      
        Who likes reading books? -> <span>Izzudin</span>
        When does he read? -> every evening
        What does he do on Saturday? -> plays football
        Who does he play with? -> <span>Nazrin</span>
        Where? -> the field near his house
      
      <p>Teacher writes the answers together with the class on the board.</p>

      <p><strong>Part B (Pair work -- worksheet):</strong> Teacher plays a second audio (read twice):</p>
      <p>
        "My name is <span>Thaqif Danial</span>. I love cycling. I cycle with my cousin <span>Thaqif Umar</span> every Sunday morning. We go to the park near the lake. I also like drawing. I draw after dinner."
      </p>
      <p>Students work in pairs to fill in this table on their worksheet:</p>
      
        
          Name
          Hobby 1
          When?
          Where?
          Hobby 2
        
        
          _______
          _______
          _______
          _______
          _______
        
      
      <p>Teacher plays audio twice. Stronger students (<span>Santhiya</span>, <span>Nick Mikhael</span>) help weaker partners.</p>
      <p>Teacher checks answers with whole class. <span>Muslimatul Ramadani</span> reads her answer for Hobby 1, <span>Thishanthan</span> reads Hobby 2, <span>Riszuwan Haiqal</span> reads When.</p>
      <p><strong>Answers:</strong> Thaqif Danial | cycling | every Sunday morning | park near the lake | drawing</p>
    

    
    Activity 3: Listen and Tick (True/False) <span>7 min</span>
    
      Individual Listening Task
      <p>Teacher gives each student a worksheet with 7 statements. Teacher reads (or plays audio of) a longer passage. Students listen and tick <strong>True (T)</strong> or <strong>False (F)</strong>.</p>
      <p>
        "Hello friends! My name is <span>Wardah Zhufairah</span>. I have many hobbies. I like watching TV and cycling. I watch TV in the evening after homework. I cycle every Saturday with my sister. My friend <span>Nur Irish Zara</span> likes painting. She paints every weekend in her room. My friend <span>Syahir Haikal</span> likes playing video games. He plays for one hour after school. He plays with his brother. We all have fun hobbies!"
      </p>
      <p><strong>Statements (on worksheet):</strong></p>
      
        Wardah likes watching TV. (T)
        She watches TV in the morning. (F -- evening)
        She cycles every Saturday. (T)
        Nur Irish Zara likes painting. (T)
        Nur Irish Zara paints every day. (F -- every weekend)
        Syahir Haikal plays video games for two hours. (F -- one hour)
        Syahir Haikal plays with his sister. (F -- his brother)
      
      <p>Teacher reads the passage <strong>twice</strong>. First read: students just listen. Second read: students tick their answers.</p>
      
        <strong>For A1 students:</strong> Teacher highlights the key words in each statement before reading ("Listen for 'Wardah' -- what does she like?"). <span>Thanantigally</span> and <span>Tharinish</span> receive worksheet with only 4 statements (easier ones: 1, 2, 3, 4).
      
      <p>Peer check: Students swap worksheets with partners to check. Teacher goes through answers orally. <span>Zulzahrin Syazani</span> reads answer for Q1, <span>Nur Indrianna Mikayla</span> for Q3, <span>Afiq Ziyad</span> for Q5.</p>
    
  

  
  
    Post-lesson <span>5 min</span>
    <p><strong>Exit Ticket: "One Thing I Heard"</strong></p>
    <p>Teacher asks: <em>"Tell me one specific detail you remember from today's listening activities."</em></p>
    <p>Students complete a simple exit slip:</p>
    <p>
      <strong>Name:</strong> _______________<br><br>
      <strong>One hobby I heard about today:</strong> _______________<br>
      <strong>One detail I remember (who / when / where):</strong> _______________
    </p>
    <p>Teacher collects slips to check understanding and identify students who need extra support.</p>
    <p><span>Harith Aqasha</span> shares one thing he heard. <span>Wafy Hau</span> shares another.</p>
    <p>Teacher closes: <em>"Excellent listening today, 1 Devotion! Tomorrow we will practice speaking about our own hobbies."</em></p>
  

  
  
    Teacher's Notes
    
      <strong>Marks context:</strong> 1D Formative M19-M Writing avg 4.2/8 (53%) -- 17 students. This listening lesson builds comprehension skills to support writing. Focus on extracting details from spoken texts will help students gather ideas before writing about their own hobbies.
    
    
      <strong>Differentiation:</strong> A1 students get 4-statement version of Activity 3 and extra pauses between sentences. A2 Revise students complete all 7 statements independently.
      <strong>Audio delivery:</strong> If no recording available, teacher reads with clear enunciation, normal speed, and natural intonation. Pause 3 seconds between sentences on second reading.
      <strong>Pairing:</strong> Mix stronger listeners (Santhiya, Nick Mikhael, Syahir Haikal) with weaker ones for Activity 2B pair work.
