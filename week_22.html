Reflection

The reading passage with real student names (Dharshayani, Ryyan, Firdaus, Wardah) was engaging -- students paid more attention when they saw their friends in the text. The picture-table comprehension strategy worked well for the A2 Revise group; most pairs could extract at least 3 out of 4 rows correctly. <p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>

<p>1. <p>1. Give each pair a worksheet with the same unpunctuated paragraph from Set Induction.
