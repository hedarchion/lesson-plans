# ISNIN (Monday)

## 1 Devotion (1D)
**Skill:** Listening | **CS:** 1.1 | **LS:** 1.1.2

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Play 3 short sound clips (ball bouncing, guitar strumming, pages turning). Ask: "What do you hear? What hobby is this?" Let students guess.
2. Introduce: "Today we listen for specific details about hobbies & free time." Write LS 1.1.2 on board.
3. Share LO: Complete a listening task with 4/7 correct.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong>
1. Pre-teach: playing football, reading, watching TV, listening to music. Say each, students repeat. Picture matching.
2. Draw a simple table (Name / Hobby). Model filling one row from a sample description.

<strong>Activity 1</strong>
1. Read 3 short descriptions (2-3 sentences each). Students tick the correct hobby from 3 choices and write one detail.
2. Pair check, then class discussion. Use sentence frames: "He/She likes ___."
3. Re-read the last description. Students listen and point to the correct picture card.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Quick recap: teacher says a description, students call out the hobby.
2. Exit ticket: Complete "I like to ___ in my free time" or draw your hobby.
3. Praise effort.</p>
```

### Reflection
Students responded positively to the hobbies & free time listening activity. Most completed the guided task with teacher support. Weaker students needed individual attention during the main task. Common vocabulary errors noted -- will reinforce in the next meeting. Overall progress toward the learning objective was satisfactory.

## 1 Endurance (1E)
**Skill:** Listening | **CS:** 1.1 | **LS:** 1.1.1

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Play a 30-sec audio of someone describing their weekend. Ask: "What did this person do? How did they feel?" Discuss.
2. "Today we practise listening for main ideas about hobbies & free time." Write LS 1.1.1.
3. LO: Identify the main idea from 3 hobby descriptions with peer discussion.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong>
1. Pre-teach: photography, hiking, collecting stamps, playing an instrument. Elicit meanings from students.
2. "Listen for the main idea first, then supporting details." Play one sample and discuss main idea vs details.

<strong>Activity 1</strong>
1. Play/read 3 longer hobby descriptions (4-5 sentences). Students identify: what hobby, and the speaker's opinion about it.
2. Pair compare. Class discussion with follow-up: "Do you agree with the speaker?"
3. Write one sentence about their own hobby using a detail from the listening.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Recap: "One new word? One thing you learned?" 2-3 students share.
2. Exit ticket: Write one sentence about what you practised today.
3. Preview next lesson.</p>
```

### Reflection
Students showed good engagement with the hobbies & free time listening task. Most completed the activity independently or with minimal support. Higher achievers contributed thoughtful responses during class discussion. The differentiated approach helped all students participate at their level. Follow-up on vocabulary extension in the next lesson.

# SELASA (Tuesday)

## 1 Devotion (1D)
**Skill:** Reading | **CS:** 3.1 | **LS:** 3.1.2

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Show a short paragraph about a hobby with 3 blanks. Ask students to guess missing words from context.
2. "Today we read about hobbies & free time and find specific details." Write LS 3.1.2.
3. Distribute reading text. LO: Answer 4 True/False questions.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong>
1. Pre-teach 4 key hobby words with picture cards. Read the short text aloud while students follow.
2. Model finding one specific detail: "Let us find what hobby Sarah likes." Read together and locate the sentence.

<strong>Activity 1</strong>
1. Students re-read the text in pairs. Answer 4 True/False questions. Correct the false ones.
2. Class check. Re-read relevant sentences for each answer.
3. Complete a simple table: Name / Hobby / Where / How Often.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Quick recap: teacher says a description, students call out the hobby.
2. Exit ticket: Complete "I like to ___ in my free time" or draw your hobby.
3. Praise effort.</p>
```

### Reflection
Students responded positively to the hobbies & free time reading activity. Most completed the guided task with teacher support. Weaker students needed individual attention during the main task. Common vocabulary errors noted -- will reinforce in the next meeting. Overall progress toward the learning objective was satisfactory.

## 1 Meekness (1M)
**Skill:** Reading | **CS:** 3.1 | **LS:** 3.1.4

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Show a picture of an unusual hobby (pottery/origami). Ask: "What is this? Would you try it? Why/why not?"
2. "Today we read about free-time activities and learn strategies to understand new words." Write LS 3.1.4.
3. LO: Read and use resources to check unfamiliar vocabulary.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong>
1. Pre-teach 6 vocabulary items (unusual hobbies). Introduce using a dictionary to check meaning.
2. Model using context clues to guess meaning before checking the dictionary.

<strong>Activity 1</strong>
1. Read the text individually. Answer 5 comprehension questions.
2. Look up 3 unfamiliar words using a dictionary/glossary. Write the meaning.
3. Pair check. Compare dictionary definitions found by different pairs. Write one new sentence with a word just learned.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Recap: "One new word? One thing you learned?" 2-3 students share.
2. Exit ticket: Write one sentence about what you practised today.
3. Preview next lesson.</p>
```

### Reflection
Students showed good engagement with the hobbies & free time reading task. Most completed the activity independently or with minimal support. Higher achievers contributed thoughtful responses during class discussion. The differentiated approach helped all students participate at their level. Follow-up on vocabulary extension in the next lesson.

## 1 Meekness (1M)
**Skill:** Writing | **CS:** 4.1 | **LS:** 4.1.1

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Show a messy first draft vs a neat final paragraph. Ask: "Which looks better? Why? What makes writing clear?"
2. "Today we plan, draft, and organise a paragraph about our free time." Write LS 4.1.1.
3. LO: Plan and write a well-organised paragraph with a partner feedback step.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong>
1. Show a model 2-paragraph text. Discuss: topic sentence, supporting details, connectors (first, also, moreover).
2. Brainstorm connectors & opinion phrases: "In my free time...", "I enjoy...", "The best part is..."

<strong>Activity 1</strong>
1. Plan 2 paragraphs (Para 1: my hobby, Para 2: why I like it) using a planning template.
2. Draft. Exchange with a partner. Peer feedback: 2 good things + 1 suggestion.
3. Revise based on feedback. Write the final version.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>

3. Preview next lesson.</p>
```

**Skill:** Writing | **CS:** 4.1 | **LS:** 4.1.3

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. On the board write jumbled words: "ikingb / mmingwis / hingis / dingrea". Students unscramble to form hobby words.

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong>
1. Show a model paragraph: "My hobby is reading. I read every night. I like adventure stories. Reading is fun." Highlight: topic sentence, details, closing.
2. Brainstorm hobby words. Write word bank + sentence frames on board.

<strong>Activity 1</strong>
1. Provide a fill-in-the-blank paragraph (8 blanks). Students complete using the word bank.
2. Copy the complete paragraph neatly. Add one more sentence of their own.
3. 2-3 volunteers read aloud. Class gives a clap.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Quick recap: teacher says a description, students call out the hobby.

3. Praise effort.</p>
```
