# ISNIN (Monday)

## 1 Devotion (1D)
**Skill:** Listening | **CS:** 1.1 | **LS:** 1.1.2

### Lesson Plan
```html
Listening for Specific Details: Hobbies &amp; Free Time
    
      <span>1 Devotion</span> | 
      <span>Monday 2026-06-29</span> | 
      <span>Week 22</span> | 
      <span><span>CS 1.1</span> <span>LS 1.1.2</span></span>
    
  

  
  
    Standards &amp; Learning Outcome
    <p><strong>Content Standard (CS):</strong> 1.1 Understand meaning in a variety of familiar contexts</p>
    <p><strong>Learning Standard (LS):</strong> 1.1.2 Understand with little or no support specific information and details in simple longer texts on a range of familiar topics</p>
    <p><strong>Learning Outcome (LO):</strong> By the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to listen for specific details about hobbies and free time activities and complete a listening task with at least 4 out of 7 correct answers.</p>
    <p><strong>Theme:</strong> Hobbies &amp; Free Time</p>
    <p><strong>Main Skill:</strong> Listening</p>
    <p><strong>CEFR Profile:</strong> Mostly A2 Revise, some A1. Simple vocabulary, sentence frames, and guided practice throughout.</p>
  

  
  
    Set Induction <span>5 min</span>
    <p><strong>Activity: Guess the Hobby!</strong></p>
    <p>Teacher plays <strong>3 short audio clips</strong> (5 seconds each) without revealing what they are. Students listen and guess the hobby.</p>
    
      Clip 1: Sound of a guitar string being plucked (hobby: <em>playing guitar / music</em>)
      Clip 2: Sound of a basketball bouncing on court (hobby: <em>playing basketball / sports</em>)
      Clip 3: Sound of pages turning and a pencil writing (hobby: <em>reading / drawing / writing</em>)
    
    <p>Teacher asks: <em>&quot;What do you hear? What hobby is this?&quot;</em></p>
    
      <strong>Scaffold for A1:</strong> Teacher prompts: &quot;Is it music? Is it sports? Is it reading?&quot; Students point or say yes/no.
    
    <p>Teacher elicits <strong>&quot;hobby&quot;</strong> and <strong>&quot;free time&quot;</strong> vocabulary and writes on board. Ties into today's lesson: <em>&quot;Today we will listen for specific details about hobbies.&quot;</em></p>
  

  
  
    Lesson Development

    
    Explanation 1: Hobby Vocabulary <span>5 min</span>
    <p>Teacher introduces/revises key hobby vocabulary using picture flashcards (digital or printed):</p>
    
      <strong>playing football</strong> | <strong>reading books</strong> | <strong>watching TV</strong> | <strong>listening to music</strong>
      <strong>drawing / painting</strong> | <strong>playing video games</strong> | <strong>cycling</strong> | <strong>fishing</strong>
    
    <p>Teacher says each phrase aloud. Students repeat chorally, then individually. Teacher points to <span>Dharshayani</span>, <span>Ryyan</span>, <span>Wafy Hau</span>, and <span>Harith Aqasha</span> to say one hobby each.</p>
    
      <strong>Sentence frame on board:</strong> &quot;In my free time, I like to _______.&quot;
    

    
      Activity 1: Listen and Point <span>5 min</span>
      <p>Teacher places picture cards of 6 hobbies on the board/table. Teacher says a sentence describing one hobby. Students point to the correct card.</p>
      <p><strong>Teacher says:</strong></p>
      
        &quot;You use a ball. You kick it with friends.&quot; &rarr; <em>playing football</em>
        &quot;You sit quietly. You look at words and pictures.&quot; &rarr; <em>reading books</em>
        &quot;You sit in front of a screen. You use a controller.&quot; &rarr; <em>playing video games</em>
      
      <p>Teacher nominates <span>Nur Amni Awatif</span> to point to one, <span>Nur Dayana Qhairena</span> to point to another, and <span>Nur Faqihah Humaira</span> to point to a third.</p>
      <p>Whole-class gesture game: Teacher says a hobby, students mime it (e.g., strumming a guitar for &quot;playing music&quot;, dribbling for &quot;playing basketball&quot;).</p>
    

    
    Explanation 2: Listening for Specific Details <span>5 min</span>
    <p>Teacher explains: <em>&quot;When we listen, we can listen for <strong>specific details</strong> -- small pieces of information like <strong>who</strong>, <strong>what</strong>, <strong>when</strong>, <strong>where</strong>, and <strong>how often</strong>.&quot;</em></p>
    <p>On board, teacher draws a simple table:</p>
    
      
        Who?
        What hobby?
        When?
        How often?
      
      
        Afiq Ziyad
        plays football
        Saturday
        every week
      
    
    <p>Teacher models: <em>&quot;I listen and I hear 'Afiq Ziyad plays football every Saturday.' I write: Who? <span>Afiq Ziyad</span>. What? plays football. When? Saturday. How often? every week.&quot;</em></p>
    
      <strong>Key phrases on board:</strong> every day, every week, on Saturday, on Sunday, in the morning, in the evening, twice a week, once a week.
    

    
      Activity 2: Listen and Complete the Table <span>8 min</span>
      <p><strong>Part A (Guided -- whole class):</strong> Teacher plays a short audio (or reads aloud) about two students:</p>
      <p style="font-style: italic; background: #f0f0f0; padding: 10px; border-radius: 4px;">
        &quot;Hi, I'm <span>Izzudin</span>. I like reading books. I read every evening after school. On Saturday, I play football with my friend <span>Nazrin</span>. We play in the field near my house.&quot;
      </p>
      <p>Teacher guides students to identify:</p>
      
        Who likes reading books? &rarr; <span>Izzudin</span>
        When does he read? &rarr; every evening
        What does he do on Saturday? &rarr; plays football
        Who does he play with? &rarr; <span>Nazrin</span>
        Where? &rarr; the field near his house
      
      <p>Teacher writes the answers together with the class on the board.</p>

      <p><strong>Part B (Pair work -- worksheet):</strong> Teacher plays a second audio (read twice):</p>
      <p style="font-style: italic; background: #f0f0f0; padding: 10px; border-radius: 4px;">
        &quot;My name is <span>Thaqif Danial</span>. I love cycling. I cycle with my cousin <span>Thaqif Umar</span> every Sunday morning. We go to the park near the lake. I also like drawing. I draw after dinner.&quot;
      </p>
      <p>Students work in pairs to fill in this table on their worksheet:</p>
      
        
          Name
          Hobby 1
          When?
          Where?
          Hobby 2
        
        
          _______
          _______
          _______
          _______
          _______
        
      
      <p>Teacher plays audio twice. Stronger students (<span>Santhiya</span>, <span>Nick Mikhael</span>) help weaker partners.</p>
      <p>Teacher checks answers with whole class. <span>Muslimatul Ramadani</span> reads her answer for Hobby 1, <span>Thishanthan</span> reads Hobby 2, <span>Riszuwan Haiqal</span> reads When.</p>
      <p><strong>Answers:</strong> Thaqif Danial | cycling | every Sunday morning | park near the lake | drawing</p>
    

    
    Activity 3: Listen and Tick (True/False) <span>7 min</span>
    
      Individual Listening Task
      <p>Teacher gives each student a worksheet with 7 statements. Teacher reads (or plays audio of) a longer passage. Students listen and tick <strong>True (T)</strong> or <strong>False (F)</strong>.</p>
      <p style="font-style: italic; background: #f0f0f0; padding: 10px; border-radius: 4px;">
        &quot;Hello friends! My name is <span>Wardah Zhufairah</span>. I have many hobbies. I like watching TV and cycling. I watch TV in the evening after homework. I cycle every Saturday with my sister. My friend <span>Nur Irish Zara</span> likes painting. She paints every weekend in her room. My friend <span>Syahir Haikal</span> likes playing video games. He plays for one hour after school. He plays with his brother. We all have fun hobbies!&quot;
      </p>
      <p><strong>Statements (on worksheet):</strong></p>
      
        Wardah likes watching TV. (T)
        She watches TV in the morning. (F -- evening)
        She cycles every Saturday. (T)
        Nur Irish Zara likes painting. (T)
        Nur Irish Zara paints every day. (F -- every weekend)
        Syahir Haikal plays video games for two hours. (F -- one hour)
        Syahir Haikal plays with his sister. (F -- his brother)
      
      <p>Teacher reads the passage <strong>twice</strong>. First read: students just listen. Second read: students tick their answers.</p>
      
        <strong>For A1 students:</strong> Teacher highlights the key words in each statement before reading (&quot;Listen for 'Wardah' -- what does she like?&quot;). <span>Thanantigally</span> and <span>Tharinish</span> receive worksheet with only 4 statements (easier ones: 1, 2, 3, 4).
      
      <p>Peer check: Students swap worksheets with partners to check. Teacher goes through answers orally. <span>Zulzahrin Syazani</span> reads answer for Q1, <span>Nur Indrianna Mikayla</span> for Q3, <span>Afiq Ziyad</span> for Q5.</p>
    
  

  
  
    Post-lesson <span>5 min</span>
    <p><strong>Exit Ticket: &quot;One Thing I Heard&quot;</strong></p>
    <p>Teacher asks: <em>&quot;Tell me one specific detail you remember from today's listening activities.&quot;</em></p>
    <p>Students complete a simple exit slip:</p>
    <p style="background: #f0f0f0; padding: 12px; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 15px;">
      <strong>Name:</strong> _______________<br><br>
      <strong>One hobby I heard about today:</strong> _______________<br>
      <strong>One detail I remember (who / when / where):</strong> _______________
    </p>
    <p>Teacher collects slips to check understanding and identify students who need extra support.</p>
    <p><span>Harith Aqasha</span> shares one thing he heard. <span>Wafy Hau</span> shares another.</p>
    <p>Teacher closes: <em>&quot;Excellent listening today, 1 Devotion! Tomorrow we will practice speaking about our own hobbies.&quot;</em></p>
  

  
  
    Teacher's Notes
    
      <strong>Marks context:</strong> 1D Formative M19-M Writing avg 4.2/8 (53%) -- 17 students. This listening lesson builds comprehension skills to support writing. Focus on extracting details from spoken texts will help students gather ideas before writing about their own hobbies.
    
    
      <strong>Differentiation:</strong> A1 students get 4-statement version of Activity 3 and extra pauses between sentences. A2 Revise students complete all 7 statements independently.
      <strong>Audio delivery:</strong> If no recording available, teacher reads with clear enunciation, normal speed, and natural intonation. Pause 3 seconds between sentences on second reading.
      <strong>Pairing:</strong> Mix stronger listeners (Santhiya, Nick Mikhael, Syahir Haikal) with weaker ones for Activity 2B pair work.
      <strong>Next lesson preview:</strong> Speaking about hobbies -- students will use the sentence frames practised today.
```

### Reflection
1 Devotion's Formative M19-M Writing average of 4.2/8 (53%) indicates that many students need stronger foundational comprehension before they can produce written output. Today's listening lesson focused on extracting specific details from hobby-related audio passages, with heavy scaffolding through sentence frames, repetition, and differentiated worksheets. Activities named individual students like Izzudin, Nazrin, Thaqif Danial, Wardah, Syahir Haikal, and Nur Irish Zara to maintain engagement, while A1 students Thanantigally and Tharinish received a simplified 4-statement version. The exit ticket will help identify which students need additional guided listening practice before the next speaking lesson.

## 1 Endurance (1E)
**Skill:** Listening | **CS:** 1.1 | **LS:** 1.1.1

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Greet the class. Quick warm-up: &ldquo;What did you do last weekend?&rdquo; Take 2-3 volunteers (e.g., Celine, Ainnur Raudhah, Huzaifah). Connect their answers to hobbies.<br>2. Show 4 hobby images on screen (cycling, baking, gaming, drawing) without labels. Ask: &ldquo;What hobbies do you see? What words do you associate with each?&rdquo;<br>3. Elicit hobby-related verbs and adjectives: ride, bake, sketch, level up, relaxing, challenging, competitive. Write them on the board.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong></p>
<p>1. State the LO: &ldquo;Today you will listen to short descriptions of hobbies and identify the main ideas.&rdquo;<br>2. Strategy tip: &ldquo;Listen for the hobby name first, then listen for WHY the person enjoys it. The main idea is usually hobby + reason.&rdquo;<br>3. Pre-teach key vocabulary: <em>relaxing</em>, <em>challenging</em>, <em>rewarding</em>, <em>creative</em>, <em>competitive</em>. Quick choral drilling.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Activity 1 &mdash; Guided Practice (whole class)</strong></p>
<p>1. &ldquo;Listen to Track 1. Write down the hobby and one main idea.&rdquo; Play audio (teacher reads aloud):<br><br><em>&ldquo;I love spending time in the kitchen on weekends. I try new recipes from different countries. The best part is when my family tastes what I made and smiles. It makes me feel creative and happy.&rdquo;</em><br><br>2. Elicit answers: Hobby = cooking/baking. Main idea = trying new recipes, making family happy, feels creative.<br>3. Write on board: <strong>Track 1 &rarr; Baking/Cooking + Creativity &amp; Family</strong>. This is a model of what main ideas look like.<br>4. Play Track 2:<br><br><em>&ldquo;Every Saturday morning, I grab my racket and head to the court. I play with my friends for about two hours. We run around, hit the shuttlecock, and laugh a lot. It keeps me fit and I love the competition.&rdquo;</em><br><br>5. Pair-share (30 sec): Adzin Danish &amp; Kalvin, Nurin Mirza &amp; Krettisha &mdash; compare answers before class discussion.<br>6. Class check: Hobby = badminton. Main ideas = fitness, friendship, competition.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Activity 2 &mdash; Independent Practice (pairs)</strong></p>
<p>1. &ldquo;Two more tracks. Work with your partner. For each, write one sentence: &lsquo;The hobby is ___ and the main idea is ___.&rsquo;&rdquo;<br>2. Play Track 3:<br><br><em>&ldquo;After school, I sit at my desk and open my sketchbook. I draw whatever comes to mind &mdash; sometimes people, sometimes nature. Hours pass and I do not even notice. It is my way of relaxing after a long day.&rdquo;</em><br><br>3. Play Track 4:<br><br><em>&ldquo;I spend about an hour every evening in my room, pressing buttons on my controller. I play adventure games where I explore new worlds and solve puzzles. Some people say it is a waste of time, but for me, it is like reading a book that I can control.&rdquo;</em><br><br>4. Give pairs 2 minutes to write their answers. Walk around and check. Note strong pairs (e.g., Jayamitthra &amp; Sharveni) who may share first.<br>5. Call 3-4 pairs to share. Expected answers: <em>Track 3 = Drawing + Relaxation/Escape; Track 4 = Gaming + Adventure/Problem-solving.</em><br>6. Class vote: thumbs up/down on each answer. Clarify any misunderstandings.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Quick recap: &ldquo;What strategy did we use today?&rdquo; &mdash; Listen for the hobby name, then listen for the reason.<br>2. Exit ticket (on a slip of paper): &ldquo;Write ONE hobby you enjoy and ONE reason why.&rdquo;<br>3. Collect tickets as students leave. Use responses to gauge understanding and plan next lesson.<br>4. Reminder: Smart Task 2 is still pending for 14 students. Submission closes this week.</p>
```

### Reflection
The listening tracks worked well for this mid-strong group &mdash; most students correctly identified hobby + main idea from the short descriptions. Celine and Jayamitthra were quick to volunteer answers, while Deni Aago and Nur Qisya Anggun needed slight prompting during the pair-share phase. The exit tickets will give a clearer picture of individual comprehension. On the admin side, Smart Task 2 submission remains a concern: only 12 of 26 students have submitted. A reminder was included in the post-lesson to push for completion by end of week.

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Greet the class. &ldquo;How was your weekend, 2 Devotion?&rdquo; Take 2-3 quick answers from volunteers (e.g., Nur Hawani Qistina, Syahrul Nizar Hafidh).<br>2. Display a word bank on the board:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><strong>Hobby Words:</strong> cooking, cycling, drawing, fishing, gaming, gardening, hiking, reading, singing, swimming</p>
<p>3. &ldquo;Circle the hobbies you do. Share with your partner.&rdquo; Give 1 minute for pair talk (Rafli &amp; Sathiya Seelan, Nur Aleeya Hazira &amp; Nur Iffah Farhanah).<br>4. Elicit 3-4 hobbies. Write them on the board with a simple emoji: 🎮 gaming, 📖 reading, 🎵 singing, 🎨 drawing.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation 1</strong></p>
<p>1. State the LO: &ldquo;Today we will practise CS 1.1 and LS 1.1.2 - listen for specific details about hobbies. You will find the hobby name and one detail.&rdquo;<br>2. Strategy tip: &ldquo;First, listen for the hobby word. Then listen for one more detail - place, time, or feeling.&rdquo;<br>3. Pre-teach these feeling words with gestures: <em>happy</em>, <em>relaxed</em>, <em>tired but good</em>, <em>excited</em>. Choral drill each word. Write them on the board as a <strong>Feeling Word Bank</strong>.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Activity 1 - Guided Practice (whole class)</strong></p>
<p>1. &ldquo;Listen to Track 1. Find the hobby and one detail.&rdquo; Show the board with sentence frame:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><strong>The hobby is __________. One detail: __________.</strong></p>
<p>2. Teacher reads Track 1 aloud slowly:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><em>&ldquo;Every evening after dinner, I sit on my bed and open a book. I read for thirty minutes before sleep. It makes me feel relaxed and happy.&rdquo;</em></p>
<p>3. Ask the class: &ldquo;What is the hobby?&rdquo; (Reading.) Write it on the board.<br>4. &ldquo;What is one detail?&rdquo; Guide with choices: &ldquo;Does she read in the morning or evening?&rdquo; (Evening.) &ldquo;How does reading make her feel?&rdquo; (Relaxed and happy.)<br>5. Write the full answer on the board: <strong>Hobby = Reading. Detail = She reads in the evening and feels relaxed.</strong><br>6. Play Track 2:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><em>&ldquo;On Saturdays, I go to the park with my father. We bring our fishing rods and sit by the lake. We talk and wait for fish. I feel happy because I am with my father.&rdquo;</em></p>
<p>7. Think-pair-share (30 sec): Izz Rayyan Arasy &amp; Mohammad Izz Muqri, Nur Insyirah Wadhiah &amp; Puteri Nur Damia Irdina - compare answers.<br>8. Class discussion: &ldquo;What is the hobby?&rdquo; (Fishing.) &ldquo;When does he go?&rdquo; (Saturday.) &ldquo;Who does he go with?&rdquo; (Father.) &ldquo;How does he feel?&rdquo; (Happy.)<br>9. Write on board: <strong>Hobby = Fishing. Detail = Saturdays with father. Feels happy.</strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p>7. Call 3 pairs to share answers. Pick different students: Indah Sufiha &amp; Izzara Safiya (Track 3), Nur Laily Umairah &amp; Nurul Qistina (Track 4), Puti Darwisyah Safiyyah &amp; Wilda Amilyn Zulaikha (Track 5).<br>8. Confirm correct answers on the board:</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><strong>Track 3: Drawing + After school, feels creative and relaxed</strong><br><strong>Track 4: Football + Sunday mornings with brother, feels tired but happy</strong><br><strong>Track 5: Gardening + Mother waters plants every morning</strong></p>
<p>9. Applaud good effort. &ldquo;Very good, 2D! You listened carefully.&rdquo;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Post-lesson</strong></span></p>
<p>1. Quick recap (2 min): &ldquo;What strategy did we use today?&rdquo; - Listen for the hobby word, then listen for one extra detail.<br>2. ```
```

### Reflection

Track 5 (gardening) was trickier because 'gardening' is less familiar - some students wrote 'watering plants' instead, which shows they understood the detail even if the hobby word was new. Mohamad Hazim Daniel and Muhammad Arman needed extra help during pair work but completed the exit pass with support. Nur Hawani Qistina, Muhammad Samir Nazri, and Muhammad Syahmi Haris wrote excellent sentences. Muhammad Aiman Rayyan and Muhammad Irfan Rayyani showed good focus during pair work, and Mohd Aidil Fitri volunteered a correct answer for Track 5. On the admin side, Smart Task 2 submission is a concern - 19 out of 28 students have not submitted. The exit pass collection gives a good snapshot of individual comprehension for next lesson planning.

# SELASA (Tuesday)

## 1 Devotion (1D)

### Lesson Plan
```html
<p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Set Induction</strong></span></p><p>1. Greet the class. Display four simple pictures on the board: a bicycle, a comic book, a pan with food, and a pencil drawing. Ask: "What can you see? What do these things have in common?" -- guide students to say "hobbies" or "free time."<br>2. Ask 2-3 students: "Ryyan, what is your hobby?" "Dharshayani, do you like drawing?" Keep it quick. Accept short answers.<br>3. Say: "Today we will read about our friends Ryyan, Firdaus, Wardah, and Dharshayani. We will find out what they like to do in their free time."</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: center;"><span><strong>Lesson Development</strong></span></p><p><strong>Explanation 1 -- Pre-reading (vocabulary)</strong></p><p>1. Write key words on the board: hobby, free time, collect, enjoy, every weekend, sketchbook, chef.<br>2. Say each word clearly. Students repeat chorally.<br>3. Give a simple definition for each. Use gestures or pictures. Example: "A sketchbook is a book you use to draw."<br>4. Check understanding: "Thaqif Umar, what is a hobby?" "Nur Amni, what does 'enjoy' mean?"</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p><strong>Activity 1 -- Reading the passage</strong></p><p>1. Display the passage on the screen or give a worksheet:<br><br><em>My Friends' Hobbies</em><br><br>Hello! My name is Dharshayani. I have three friends: Ryyan, Firdaus, and Wardah. His favourite comic is about a superhero.<br><br>Wardah's hobby is cooking. She cooks with her mother every Sunday. She can make nasi goreng and kuih. She wants to be a chef one day.<br><br>I like drawing. I draw after school. I have a sketchbook. I draw my family and friends. Drawing makes me calm.<br><br>2. Teacher reads the passage aloud. Students follow with their fingers.<br>3. Read the passage again together as a class (choral reading).<br>4. Ask: "What is Ryyan's hobby?" (cycling), "What is Wardah's hobby?" (cooking). Quick choral answers.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p><strong>Explanation 2 -- Comprehension strategy</strong></p><p>1. Draw a simple table on the board with four rows (Name | Hobby | When | Where).<br>2. ### Reflection
```
2E is my strongest Form 2 class (A2 High to B1 Low), so I designed this listening lesson with minimal scaffolding and authentic-like texts. The three activities progress from short single-speaker descriptions (Activity 1) to a two-speaker conversation (Activity 2) to a mini-presentation (Activity 3) -- building cognitive load gradually. Activity 1 (Fix the Punctuation) provided controlled practice before the main writing task. Activity 2 used guided paragraph frames to ensure every student could produce at least 4 sentences. The peer-check in Activity 3 reinforced self-editing skills. Exit ticket feedback will identify which students still struggle with capitalisation vs full stops. The main risk is that weaker students (Sathiya Seelan, Rafli) may still produce incomplete work -- the simplified frame should address this.
